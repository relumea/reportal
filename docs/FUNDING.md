# Funding

reportal is a private SaaS project (Singapore + US entities, beta with pilot
users). It is not open source, so OSS foundation grants are out of scope.
The targets below are non-dilutive grants first, then accelerators and
pre-seed investors.

## Blocker: license

`pyproject.toml` still declares `license = "MIT"` and the repo is public at
`github.com/relumea/reportal`. The Singapore grants below require proprietary
IP owned and exploited from Singapore. Before applying: switch the license to
proprietary and make the repo private.

## 1. Startup SG Tech (Singapore): best fit now

Up to S$250,000 (POC) or S$500,000 (POV) via Enterprise Singapore.
reportal fits POV: technically viable, beta with pilots, building the working
prototype toward commercial validation.

Requirements: SG-registered under 5 years, at least 30 percent SC/PR
shareholding, core activities in Singapore, not a subsidiary, and the project
must not have started at application time. Frame the application around the
next module (for example AI triage plus SBOM export), not work already done.

Terms: 10 percent (POC) or 20 percent (POV) paid-up capital top-up, and ESG
may subscribe for 50 percent of the grant amount at the next qualifying raise
(up to 49 percent of shares). Process is Registration of Interest, invited
formal application, expert panel; it takes months, so start now.

* [Official application page](https://www.startupsg.gov.sg/programmes/4897/startup-sg-tech/apply)
* [Grant summary](https://grants.sg/grants/startup-sg-tech)

## 2. CSA CCDF via CyberCall (Singapore): biggest cheque

Up to S$1,000,000 over 24 months for manpower and operating expenditure,
reimbursement basis. Binary analysis and malware triage map directly onto CII
and strategic stakeholder challenges.

Requirements: SG-registered company (or partner with one), IP owned, managed
and exploited from Singapore, an end-user co-applicant (evaluation weights
end-user commitment), and a project that has not started.

The 2025 call is closed; the 2026 challenge statements land on CyberCall.
Move now: email `cybercall@csa.gov.sg`, join the mailing list, and convert one
pilot into an LOI as the end-user co-applicant.

* [CCDF fund page](https://www.csa.gov.sg/our-programmes/innovation-schemes/csa-cybersecurity-co-innovation-and-development-fund/)
* [CyberCall programme](https://tig.cybersg.sg/program/innovation/cybercall/)
* [CyberCall challenges](https://cybercall.sg/?trk=organization_guest_main-feed-card_reshare-text)

## 3. PSG pre-approval (Singapore): sales lever

Under [PSG Cybersecurity Solutions](https://www.csa.gov.sg/our-programmes/support-for-enterprises/psg-cybersecurity-solutions/),
a pre-approved reportal lets SG SME customers claim about 50 percent subsidy
on the purchase, which turns pilots into paid conversions. Apply through
[IMDA pre-approval](http://www.imda.gov.sg/icmvendors) after the Startup SG
Tech filing is in. Grant stacking background is
[here](https://infinitecybersecurity.com/articles/csa-cybersecurity-grants-singapore-sme-funding).

## 4. NSF SBIR Phase I Cybersecurity (US entity)

About USD 275,000 to 305,000 Phase I award, non-dilutive, under the
Cybersecurity and Authentication topic. Requires a US small business (under
500 staff, US owned and operated), so apply through the US entity. Slow (6 or
more months) but a strong signal for angels.

* [NSF SBIR/STTR Cybersecurity 2026](https://grantedai.com/grants/nsf-small-business-innovation-research-sbir-small-business-technology-tr-national-science-foundation-nsf-f05d693d#1)

## 5. Accelerators

* CyberBoost NEXT / Cyber Venture Accelerator (Singapore, 12 weeks, first
  week in person): built for cyber startups expanding in Singapore and APAC.
  The 2026 round ran 10 April to 8 May 2026, so target 2027. Track the
  [Innovation programme](https://tig.cybersg.sg/program/innovation/).
* Y Combinator: AI security dominated the W26 demo day and devtools/security
  is a standing batch. Apply with pilot revenue. See
  [YC cybersecurity](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/industry/cybersecurity)
  and [YC DevSecOps](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/industry/devsecops).
